Questions people ask first

I'm not technical. Can we still work together?

Yes. Describe the problem in your own words and I handle the technical side, explaining each choice in plain language.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short conversation I send a written quote, so you know the price before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

A simple business website often takes one to three weeks. Web apps and automation take longer, and I give you a timeline in the quote.

What if I only have a rough idea?

That is a good place to start. We shape the idea together into a small first version you can test before investing more.

Will the site work on phones?

Yes. I build responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktops, and test the experience across screen sizes.

What happens after launch?

I can help with fixes, content updates and new features after your site or tool goes live.

Have a problem that software could solve?

Tell me what is slowing your business down, who it affects and what a better outcome would look like. If you have a budget or deadline in mind, include that too.

Example: "We take orders on WhatsApp and lose track of them."
